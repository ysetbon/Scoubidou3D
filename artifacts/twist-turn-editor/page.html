Set each level's turn yourself.

The twist turns every level by the same angle, on the 1×1. Here each level gets its own slider: Level L is how far level L is turned from the level below. The column rebuilds as you move them, placed by the box rules, and the table reads every level back off the ribbons: crossings, arms too short to reach across, and ribbons passing through each other. When it builds clean to level 10, press Copy and paste it to me. Your settings stay in this browser if you leave the page.
