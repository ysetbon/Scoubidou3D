The box, up to ten levels, from what you placed.

Level 1 is the box from box + strand. Levels 2, 3 and 4 are exactly what you placed in the level editors, and they turned out to be one rule, which is what carries the box up to level 10. The 2×1 uses the same rules to see whether the idea holds across a face. Pick a face and a level; the table reads every crossing and fold back off the built ribbons.

The rule

Level 1The box from box + strand: vertical arms rest on −1, the slant underneath drops to −3, an arm over a slant sits on 0, the arm underneath at an arm crossing sits on 0.
Every level aboveArms rest on −1. At each crossing the arm on top goes to +1 and the arm underneath to −1.
Every foldThe arm below ends on −1 and the arm above starts on +1, so the turn rises clear instead of climbing through the arm beside it.
