How to read the table

Heights are in rungs, half a thickness each, off the middle of the storey. Ids are the reference's: the family numbers the weft's two arms the other way round, so its 1_2 is shown as 1_3 and back. Green is within half a rung of the reference with the same lace on top. Amber is not.

What is the same

Every placement, one for one: the two arm planes at −1 and the nine crossing placements, once the arm ids are matched. The weave is the same at all four arm crossings, and the vertical slant is on top.

What is not

The geometry and the colours. The reference was drawn with skewed arms and 54 px laces and a yellow vertical set; the family is square with 46 px laces, so a crossing lands in a slightly different spot and the heights cannot match to the last digit. Whether to take the reference's width and angles for the 1×1 is the open question.
