What level 1 taught

Your box places four things. The vertical arms rest on rung −1. The slant underneath drops to −3 and the one on top sits on the middle. An arm over a slant sits on the middle. Where two arms cross, the arm underneath sits on the middle and the arm on top is left free, and it rises about one and a half rungs by itself.

What level 2 has

No slants, only the four arms carried on as _4 and _5, crossing each other four times. Each arm goes over one and under the other, and the over/under flips from level 1, as a box's has to. Your plan rests every one of them on −1, waves it up to +1 over one arm and down to −1 under the next, and starts it on +1 where it folds out of level 1.

Reading the tables

Heights are in rungs, half a thickness each, off the middle of the storey. Air is what is left between two ribbons, in thicknesses. Green is clear. Plain is touching or slightly overlapping. Amber overlaps by more than half a thickness. Ids are the family's; level 1's check uses the sample's ids.
