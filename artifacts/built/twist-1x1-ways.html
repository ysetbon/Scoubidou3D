Three ways to turn the 1×1, side by side.

The current build turns every level and puts each fold tip 60 px out from the centre. The MXN engine's own rings are the same rule at the same 56 px gap but turn 72.9° into level 2, then about 60.6°, with tips 38 and 48 px out. A tip only clears the arm it crosses from 51 px out, so the engine's rings put the fold ends inside that arm, and the ribbons break through each other there. Tips just clear is the steepest turn that avoids it. Pick a preset, or move any level's slider, and read the fold clearance off the ribbons. Copy hands back the exact turns and tips.
