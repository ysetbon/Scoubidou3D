The rules

Level 1 is the box + strand box: vertical arms rest on −1, the slant underneath drops to −3, an arm over a slant sits on 0, the arm underneath at an arm crossing sits on 0. Every level above is the plan you placed: all four arms rest on +1, and at each crossing the arm on top goes to +1 and the arm underneath to −1.

Levels

Levels are what the studio's Level panel counts: 10 levels is the starting stitch with nine rounds over it, as in the Box stitch — 10 levels sample.

Reading the ledger

For each woven crossing the page takes the height of the lace the weave calls over and the one it calls under, in thicknesses. About a thickness means they rest cleanly on each other. Less means they share some height. Negative means the lace that should ride over is the lower one.
