How the box was placed

The box in box + strand carries two maps: planes and crossPlanes. Read as roles, they say four things. Each round is placed the same way on its own storey.

  1. The vertical (warp) arms rest on the lower half, rung −1.
  2. Where the two slants cross, the one underneath drops to −3 and the one on top comes to the middle.
  3. An arm passing over a slant sits on the middle, rung 0.
  4. Where two arms cross, the one underneath sits on the middle. The one on top keeps its run.

Two things are added to that, because the hand-placed box is one short storey and these are not. The slant an arm passes over is placed on the floor, −2, since a long slant crossed by many arms otherwise rises to meet them. And once there is a storey above, the arm on top is placed at +2, since nothing lets it rise by itself any more.

Levels

Levels are what the studio's Level panel counts, so 10 levels is the starting stitch with nine rounds over it, the same as the Box stitch — 10 levels sample. 1 level is the starting stitch alone, which is the box in box + strand without its extra strand.

Reading the ledger

For each woven crossing the page takes the height of the lace the weave calls over and the one it calls under, at the crossing, in thicknesses. About a full thickness means they rest cleanly on each other. Less means they share some height. Negative means the lace that should ride over is the lower one. A hand-placed box squeezes its crossings on purpose, so squeezed is not wrong, but inverted would be.
