Where level 3 turns into level 4

At the end of each level-3 arm the ribbon turns back and carries on as a level-4 arm. Set where the arm below ends and where the arm above starts; rest keeps the arm's own plane. The line under each card says how close the turning end is to the arm beside it. Fold end points the camera at one.
