What touches

A fold stops 32 px past the neighbouring arm's centre. The neighbour is 46 wide, so its edge is only 9 px from the turning end, and a ribbon turning back needs about half a thickness (13 px) to curl. At the same time the tip is only about 12 px from the neighbour's height, and a ribbon is 26 thick.

Stops out

Moves every fold stop further out. Nothing is re-placed: the rungs are the same, and the crossings stay where they are. The box gets wider by twice the move.

Tips lifted

Keeps the footprint and lifts the last stretch of each folding arm a full thickness. This is a preview: a saved scene holds one rung per arm and cannot say a ramp, so building it in would need a new field.

Reading the figures

For every arm that has a level above it, the page takes its turning end and the nearest arm on the same level, and reads two distances off the built ribbons: from the end to that arm's near edge as seen from above, and in height. Touching means under 13 px from the edge and under 26 px in height. The picture is the judgement, so use Fold end.
