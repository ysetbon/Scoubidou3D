What this replaces

Today a crossing swings each lace off a single storey plane by weaveAmplitude, capped at half a thickness once the scene has levels. The swing is per-crossing and local; nothing records where a part rests between its crossings.

A declared plane is the opposite: it holds along the whole run, so two parts that never cross still have a defined relationship — which is exactly the case the seam is made of.
