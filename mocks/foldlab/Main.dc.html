Fold Lab

Bight Lab asked how one turn climbs. This asks where every part of every strand rests. A level stops being one plane with a swing on it and becomes three named planes — bottom, center, top — one strand thickness apart. Every part declares its plane at every level; the fold is what carries it between them.
