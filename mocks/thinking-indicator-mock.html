What this swaps out

The wheel goes

Today a busy run with no progress frame yet shows .calculation-orbit.is-spinning — a wheel turning against an empty page. It reports nothing except that the tab has not frozen.

Cheap knots instead

Each tile is one candidate drawn small, with no labels and no masking pass — a few dozen strokes on a canvas. It costs about what a repaint costs, so it can keep pace with the search.

The dots do the waiting

The plaque reads thinking . → .. → ... on its own beat. It stays legible even when the tiles behind it churn, and it is the one thing that keeps moving if the engine stalls.

Honest, not decorative

Tiles are real search output, so their rate is the search rate. A slow band visibly slows the sheet. Reduced motion drops the pop and slows the tick; the counter still advances.
