# The contact sheet

[targets.md](targets.md) says *who*. This says *where to write, and what to say
in the first line*. Everything here was checked in August 2026 and is a route the
organisation publishes itself — a company `info@`, a published sales contact, an
academic address, a lab address. **Nothing here is a guessed email pattern.**
Where only a masked address existed on a data-broker site, this file gives you
LinkedIn instead, because guessing `firstname@` is how a first impression becomes
a spam folder.

Ordered by who to write to **first**, not by company size.

## 1 · Gali Cnaani — the single best email you can send

**`gali.cn@campus.technion.ac.il`**

She is one person standing in three of your target institutions at once:
faculty at **Shenkar**, researcher at the **CodedMatter Lab at the Technion**,
and she teaches jewellery, textile and fashion design at **Bezalel**. She is also
the co-author of the adjustable weaving reed — the one that varies weft density
across the fabric and lets the dents move during weaving.

Why she is first: her reed makes fabric that **no weaving draft can represent**,
because a draft is an orthogonal grid and her whole contribution is leaving the
grid. Your studio places ribbons of real width and thickness at arbitrary
headings. You are the drawing tool for the thing she built the loom part for.

> **First line:** "You made weft density a variable across the width, and every
> drawing tool I know of still assumes an orthogonal grid. I built one that
> doesn't — strands are ribbons with real thickness and they can sit at any
> heading. Could I show you? I'm in Israel."

**Build the picture before you send it.** One render of a non-orthogonal,
variable-density weave made in your own studio is worth the entire email.

## Two findings from this pass that change the map

**Kanfit does not braid.** Israel's most visible advanced-composites shop
(Migdal HaEmek) does automated fibre placement, robotic filament winding and
additive — and hosts Israel's AFP national knowledge centre. Filament winding is
one strand laid down, not many crossing. It is not a lead, and neither is any
Israeli composites house found on the same basis. Stop looking there.

**There appears to be no Israeli braiding contract manufacturer.** Three separate
searches for Israeli nitinol braiding, braided sleeves and industrial braiders
returned nothing local — every specialist braider that surfaced is American,
German or Irish (Confluent, Nordson, ViaMed, Integer, Aran, US BioDesign).

That second one is not a dead end, it is **your opening line**. If Israeli
neurovascular companies have no local braider, then the interlacement decision is
being made *inside* Rapid Medical, Perflow, VGS and LuSeed — by their own
engineers — and then sent abroad to be made. The people who choose the pattern
are exactly the people you can reach by train, and they are choosing without a
tool that enumerates the options.
