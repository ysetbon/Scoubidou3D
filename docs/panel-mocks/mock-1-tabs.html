Tabbed panel

Six stacked sections become three peer tabs, and only one is on screen at a time — so the layer stack gets the panel's whole height instead of living below a scroll of sliders. The footer keeps the scene's name and the two camera shots pinned. Every note, hint and colour legend the panel used to print moves behind the ? in the header.

Navigation Three doors, no scrolling to find one Layers, Ribbon, Scene. The count on the Layers tab means you never open it to see whether anything is in there.
Prose All of it behind one round button The tool notes, the weave explanation, the level definition and the gesture list — one sheet, one entry per topic.
Site match Cream paper, coral rules, pill controls Same tokens as the landing page — Georgia headings, hairline dividers, hard offset shadows — and a dark theme built from the same hues.

About

Weave

Click the strand that goes over, then the one it goes under. They interlock at their crossing — the 3D version of an OpenStrand mask.

Levels

Level 0 is the ground. Every level above it is a step of one whole storey, so a lace up there rests on the woven round below instead of sinking into it.

Control points

Pull the green triangle to bend a strand; that brings out the circle and the square. Park the circle back on the start to fold them away.

Camera

Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. On a touch screen: one finger orbits, pinch zooms, two fingers pan.

Files

Opens OpenStrand Studio and OpenStrandJS saves. Save keeps a scene in this browser; Copy JSON gives you the text to share.
