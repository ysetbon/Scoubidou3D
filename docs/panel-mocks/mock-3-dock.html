Layers only, settings in a dock

The settings leave the panel altogether. Ribbon, Weave, View and Scene become four pills in a dock along the bottom of the canvas, each opening one popover next to the thing it changes — and each printing its value on the pill. The panel is then nothing but the stack: one card per level, masks in their own card, and the selected strand's colour and width opening inside its own row.

Navigation A card per storey The stack stops being one long list — you move a whole level, or fold it, from its own header.
Prose One ? over the canvas Top right, where a viewer looks for it. Panel and dock carry no sentences at all.
Site match Popovers as paper cards Hard offset shadows, gold storey badges, dashed border on the mask card — the site's whole kit.

About

The five tools

Pan slides the camera · Orbit turns it · Move drags endpoints · Attach grows a strand from a free end · Weave masks one over another.

Levels

One whole storey — the strand thickness plus the band the weave needs — so a lace up there rests on the round below instead of sinking into it.

Masks

Click the strand that goes over, then the one it goes under. With no mask on a crossing, the higher layer wins.

Camera

Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan.

Files

Reads OpenStrand Studio and OpenStrandJS saves. Everything stays in your browser.
