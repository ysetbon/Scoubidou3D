Numbered cards

The landing page's 01 / 02 / 03 band, brought into the panel. Settings collapse into three numbered cards and each one prints its own current value on its header — so the whole setup of a scene reads at a glance, and you only open the one you mean to change. Below a hard rule, the layer stack is permanent: it is the workspace, not a section.

Navigation State on the header, not inside “46 thick · ×1.00 wide · outlined” answers the question most openings were for.
Prose One underlined link, bottom left Reads like the site's own links, and it is the only sentence left in the panel.
Site match Serif numerals and titles Georgia over the grotesque controls, coral numerals, cream fill on whichever card is open — in the light theme or the dark one.

About & gestures

01  Scene

Opens OpenStrand Studio and OpenStrandJS saves. Save keeps a scene in this browser; Copy JSON gives you the text to share.

02  Ribbon

Thickness is the lace itself; width scale stretches it sideways. Both are global — every strand in the scene moves together.

03  Weave

Depth is how far a lace lifts over or dips under a crossing — the same either way, however far apart the two layers are.

Layers

Top is front. Level 0 is the ground; each level above it is one whole storey up, so a lace there rests on the woven round below instead of sinking into it.
